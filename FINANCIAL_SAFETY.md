# Financial safety contract

1. **No certainty theater.** Never describe an uncertain forecast as guaranteed, point-perfect, risk-free, or certain.
2. **Freshness first.** Record source, timestamp, market status, currency, reporting period, and evidence cutoff.
3. **Product-specific risk.** Apply the warrant, fund, equity, crypto, or Turkey-market owner before comparison.
4. **Decision gates.** A recommendation is incomplete without counter-thesis, invalidation, sizing, costs, liquidity, and an exit/review plan.
5. **No autonomous execution.** Never place an order, use brokerage credentials, bypass confirmation, or store secrets.
6. **Forecast accountability.** Freeze predictions before maturity and score them against a naive or market benchmark after the outcome.
7. **Education over dependency.** Show calculations and assumptions so the user can independently challenge the result.

This repository provides research workflows, not personalized regulated financial advice. Laws and product rules vary by jurisdiction and date.
