# Quality evidence

Prefer observable measures:

- Technical specifications that affect the use case
- Independent standardized tests
- Reliability, defect, return or warranty evidence
- Support response and service-level terms
- Verified long-term ownership cost
- Expert assessment with disclosed method and conflicts
- Large, representative user evidence after fake-review and survivorship checks

Separate:

- Measured quality
- Feature completeness
- Convenience/service
- Brand perception
- Marketing claim
- Unknown

Never award quality points solely for price, popularity or prestige.
