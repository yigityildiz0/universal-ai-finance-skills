# Pricing method

## Normalize

- Same geography and decision date
- Same currency with timestamped FX
- Tax-inclusive versus tax-exclusive
- Shipping, installation, accessories, financing and mandatory fees
- Unit, weight, volume, seat, usage, storage or contract duration
- Introductory discount versus renewal price
- Return, warranty, support and switching costs

## Compare

Use three separate views:

1. `Landed price`: cash cost to obtain and use the offer.
2. `Comparable unit price`: landed price divided by standardized quantity or service period.
3. `Value`: measured quality/features relative to comparable price, with the formula shown.

Do not collapse every attribute into one score unless weights are explicit and sensitivity is shown.

## Business pricing

For seller-side strategy, add customer segment, alternative, willingness-to-pay evidence, unit economics, gross margin, channel fees, churn, price elasticity hypothesis and competitor response. Label untested assumptions and propose the smallest ethical experiment.
