# Health-product evidence framework

## Identity record

Capture:

- manufacturer/brand and exact name;
- variant/model/form and market;
- active ingredients/components and exact concentration, strength, material, or device parameters;
- pack size, dose count, consumables, lot/expiry where relevant;
- regulatory category and claimed intended use;
- source image/page and date.

An unresolved identity limits the result to a conditional class-level analysis.

## Evidence directness ladder

1. Exact finished product, exact dose/settings, target population, patient-important outcome
2. Exact finished product with a surrogate or adjacent population
3. Same active ingredient/component and comparable dose/formulation
4. Broader product class or mechanism
5. Manufacturer theory, testimonials, before/after images, or unsupported marketing

Do not upgrade levels 3–5 into direct product effectiveness. Report where the best evidence sits.

## Clinical evidence questions

- Was the product identity verified and the study prospectively registered?
- Was the comparison fair and outcome meaningful to the user?
- Are allocation, blinding, missing data, selective reporting, funding, sample size, and follow-up adequate?
- Is the effect large enough to matter, and are harms/withdrawals reported?
- Does the evidence apply to this indication, age, comorbidity, medication, stage, and setting?
- Are there independent replications, systematic reviews, guidelines, safety alerts, recalls, or contrary studies?

## Commercial evidence questions

- Who owns the page and who earns from a sale or lead?
- Is content sponsored, affiliate-linked, coupon-driven, or written by the seller/issuer?
- Are several “reviews” tracing to one press release, study, or content network?
- Are rankings based on disclosed testing or only feature lists and merchant links?
- Does a claim omit denominator, comparator, dose, duration, or exact citation?

## Turkish practical status

For Türkiye, verify the responsible current official source appropriate to the category. Keep these separate:

- legal/regulatory classification or registration;
- prescription/dispensing status;
- SGK reimbursement and the exact indication/report/quantity conditions;
- current retail or reimbursed price;
- availability and seller authenticity.

State the effective/check date. Do not infer SGK coverage merely from an eczane listing or reimbursement in another country.

## Value comparison

Normalize total cost by useful course, dose, session, month, or expected service life. Add consumables, accessories, shipping, maintenance, warranty, return friction, and failure risk. Compare only alternatives serving the same goal and safety constraints; include no-purchase or a simpler noncommercial option when genuinely equivalent.

## Decision language

- `ALMAYA DEĞER`: credible benefit and fit justify the current cost.
- `KOŞULLU`: sensible only for a named use, population, price, supervision, or after a specific verification.
- `GEREKSİZ/DAHA İYİ ALTERNATİF VAR`: benefit is weak, mismatched, duplicative, or dominated by a better option.
- `YETERSİZ BİLGİ`: exact identity, safety context, current status, or decisive evidence is missing.
